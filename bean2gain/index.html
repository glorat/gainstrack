bean2gain

Your beancount ledger, loaded by beancount itself and handed back as JSON — with lot booking, elided amounts, includes and pad already resolved.

Nothing is uploaded. The Python interpreter and beancount are downloaded once and run inside this tab; your ledger never leaves the machine.
